Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tally-Link ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, stores and protects the information you provide when you use the Tally-Link and Tally-Link User applications and related services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide the service, we process the following data:

  • Account information: your registration ID, username and related account details.
  • Tally business data: price lists, stock details, partner balances, daybook/voucher entries and other data you choose to sync from your Tally software.
  • Usage information: basic technical data needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How We Use Information

  • To sync and display your Tally data across the Tally-Link and Tally-Link User apps.
  • To manage registrations, users and access permissions.
  • To provide support and improve the service.

3. Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in Google Sheets and Google Cloud services and is accessed only through authenticated, HTTPS-protected connections. We use Google's service-account authentication so that no password is shared with third parties. We follow reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted providers to operate the service, including Google (Google Sheets, Google Cloud) and Cloudflare (edge hosting). These providers process data only to deliver the service and are subject to their own privacy and security commitments.

5. No Sharing of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We do not share your data with third parties except as necessary to operate the service or as required by law.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your registration is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us; we will delete the data within a reasonable period unless we are required to retain it by law.

7. Your Rights

You may access, correct or request deletion of your personal data. You may also revoke access to your account at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

Tally-Link is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Whenever this page is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will also be updated. Continued use of the service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at tally.link.app@gmail.com.
